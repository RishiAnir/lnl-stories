# BULKWAY — Part 1, "After Close"

Vertical 1080×1920, 30 fps, 80 s (revision 2). Flat vector cartoon with 6-head characters (look defined in `../STYLE_LOCK.md`). Narrated in second person by a calm low male voice (Kokoro `am_michael`, speed 1.15), generated in five chunks and laid out with fixed 0.2 / 0.4 s pauses. The two-column script (Spoken / Caption) is in `lines.json`.

## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **The countdown of certainties** (chosen). Each beat removes one safety: the roads, the power, the freezers, the doors, the signal, the fuel. The "safe" stats from the first act are counted back against the crew in the office. The last certainty to go is "we're alone". It's a tightening list that ends on a turn.
2. **Cold open on CAM 06**, then "six hours earlier". It hooks harder, but it spends the reveal up front and the series needs the reveal to be the Part 1 cliffhanger.
3. **One continuous night clock**: a 10:58 → 04:37 timestamp ticks in the corner of every shot. Clear, but it turns every shot into a data screen and fights the 1–3 word captions.

Chosen: **1**. The brief's script is already built this way. The structure makes the one comic beat (the mattress flop) the high point the rest falls from, and it gives the cam reveal maximum contrast.

## 2. Logline and arc

Three night-crew workers get snowed into a warehouse club, and as the storm takes away their exits one by one they learn they were never alone.
Setup (lines 1–4, warm, confident) → turn (lines 5–11, power fails and exits close) → ending (lines 12–16, the camera grid finds a fourth person; cliffhanger).

## 3. Benchmark

- **Learn from** illustrated "what would you do" survival shorts on vertical feeds, and from the flat graphic staging of *Over the Garden Wall*-era limited TV animation: one readable idea per frame, a still body with one moving part, colour temperature as the act structure (warm → cold).
- **Don't take** any channel's characters, faces, logos, caption styling, music or specific shots.

## 6. Cue map

No tempo grid. It's a drone score that moves in sections, and the cuts follow the voice.
- **Act 1 (0:00–0:27)**: a warm low drone (A1 + E2, soft), store hum, wind faint behind glass.
- **Act 2 (0:27–0:58)**: the drone detunes and drops. At the freezer cut-out the bed stops (the voice carries on) until the generator thunk starts a low rumble bed. The wind gets louder.
- **Act 3 (0:58–end)**: the drone thins to one low tone, and a high cold sine shimmer comes in at the grid. On "Camera six" the drone drops lower (a slow pitch fall). After line 16, hard silence of about 1.2 s, then the end card's single low hit.

## 8. Subtitle and title design

Burned-in word captions, 1–3 words, synced to the voice: Archivo Black uppercase, white, thin dark stroke and soft shadow, keyword in #F28C28, centred at 60 % height. The width is clamped so the captions never enter the right 14 % or the bottom 18 %. UI cards use Inter with IBM Plex Mono for camera labels. Settings are in `../STYLE_LOCK.md`.
