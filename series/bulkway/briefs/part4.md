## Summary
The bay door is forced up an inch. Ray warns the truck crew has found them. The team barricades the dock with three pallets of water using the forklift. Ray reveals he heard the crew planning the robbery before the storm: a plow truck dug down to the dock while the roads were closed. Midway, the keypad by the bay door beeps from outside — someone is entering Dana's alarm code. The page with that code is missing from Ray's notebook, though he swears he never removed it. The door shudders again, pallets groan and begin to slide as the intruders push through. The crew faces a full breach.
