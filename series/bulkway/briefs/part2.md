## Summary
A quarter to five in the morning, the crew finds a man eating kibble in aisle twenty-two. Ray says he hid at closing, and the camera log at ten fifty-eight proves he was inside before Dana locked up. They argue about trusting him while the freezers scream and thaw, so Marcus lifts the bay door one foot and they forklift frozen pallets into the snow drift, which works as a freezer. On day three at six in the morning the generator dies a day early and the fuel can is bone dry. Cliffhanger: four flashlights in the dark, nobody speaking, and the question of who took three days of fuel.
