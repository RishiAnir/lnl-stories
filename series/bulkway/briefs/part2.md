## Summary
Aisle twenty-two is empty, but camera six still shows the man sitting there. Marcus finds a nest on the top shelf, with a notebook tracking all three of their shifts and Dana's alarm code. Camera six's clock is frozen at 4:37: someone looped the feed. Then the generator dies two days early, the fuel can is empty and still wet, and a voice behind you in the dark says not to open the bay door. Nobody's there.
