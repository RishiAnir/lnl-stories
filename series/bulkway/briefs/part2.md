## Summary
The crew finds Ray hiding in the pet food aisle since closing. After a tense standoff, he explains he had nowhere to go. Meanwhile, thousands of pounds of frozen food begin to thaw. Marcus opens the loading bay door and uses the snow drift as a makeshift freezer, forklifting pallets outside. On day three, the generator dies a day early, and the fuel can is empty — someone drained it.
