# Snowed In at Bulkway: story bible

## Premise
A blizzard buries a Bulkway Warehouse Club store with three night-shift workers inside. They are not alone: a man has been secretly living in the store for weeks, and he knows why the storm makes tonight dangerous. Outside, a crew with a plow truck is digging down to the loading dock to rob the warehouse while the roads are closed and no police can come. Mystery first, then a siege. Second person: the viewer is "you", the night-shift stocker. Five parts over about three nights.

## Characters
- **You**: early 20s night stocker. Orange knit beanie (signature), navy store vest over a gray hoodie, NIGHT CREW name tag, box cutter. Practical, notices details. Never named.
- **Dana**: late 50s night manager. Gray bob, glasses on a cord, red fleece, big key ring. Calm, does the math out loud, the one in charge.
- **Marcus**: tall forklift driver. Short beard, yellow hi-vis vest, ear defenders. Few words, strong, solves physical problems.
- **Ray**: 40s, gray-green hoodie with the hood always up, stubble. Lost his apartment in October and has been living in the store ever since, in a nest on top of the racking in aisle twenty-two, coming down at night. He knows the store better than the staff: the camera system, every crawlspace, the roof hatch. He looped camera six, drained the generator so the store would go dark, and kept a notebook of the crew's shifts so he could avoid them. Before the storm he overheard the truck crew planning the robbery in the parking lot. Not a villain: scared, secretive, and in the end the reason they survive. Seen only as a voice and a shape in Part 2; caught in Part 3.
- **The truck crew** (Part 4+): three or four men in dark winter gear with a yellow plow truck and headlamps. Never seen clearly: headlights through the bay door gap, shapes, voices.

## Outline
1. **The storm** (Night 1, already posted): storm hits, generator kicks in, doors buried, 3 days of fuel, the freezers start dripping. Cliffhanger: camera six shows someone sitting in the pet food aisle (aisle twenty-two) who was inside before close.
2. **The nest** (Night 1, about 4:40 to 5:30 AM): aisle twenty-two is empty, but Dana's screen still shows him sitting there. Marcus finds a nest on the top shelf: sleeping bag, food, store hoodies, and a notebook with all three of their shifts (a doodle of your orange beanie instead of your name) and Dana's alarm code. Camera six's clock is frozen at 4:37: someone looped the feed. The generator dies two days early; the fuel can is empty and still wet. Cliffhanger: a voice right behind you in the dark: "Don't open the bay door." Nobody's there.
3. **Ray** (Night 2): they set a trap with the nest and catch him: Ray. He admits everything (living here since October, the loop, the fuel: "the lights could be seen from the road"). From who? He won't say. Midway twist: he knows Dana's name from before; he used to work here. Cliffhanger: at two in the morning, someone knocks on the buried bay door. Three slow knocks.
4. **The truck** (Night 2 to 3): Ray finally talks: before the storm he heard men in the lot planning to empty the warehouse while the roads are closed. They've dug down to the dock with a plow truck; headlights through the gap. The crew barricades the dock with forklifts and pallets of water. Midway twist: the keypad by the bay door beeps from outside. The page with Dana's alarm code is missing from Ray's notebook, and Ray swears he never took it out. Cliffhanger: the pallets start sliding as the bay door is forced up.
5. **Morning** (Night 3 to dawn): the siege. Ray leads them through the routes only he knows (crawlspace, roof hatch) and they lock the crew in the bay with the plow truck's own chain. Final twist, then plows and police lights at dawn. Final image: the front doors slide fully open.

## Story rules
- Open every part on its hook: the strangest or most dangerous fact, in the first sentence. Part 2 opens on aisle twenty-two being empty while the camera still shows him there.
- Something happens every few seconds: actions, dialogue, texts, reveals. No supply lists, no weather recaps, no backstory paragraphs.
- The characters talk. Dana gives orders and does math out loud in quotes, Marcus says little but it lands, Ray is evasive and scared. Let them argue.
- A twist midway through each part, and a twist plus a question at the end.
- Survival detail only inside an event (forklifting pallets into the drift, not explaining why snow keeps food cold).
- At most two numbers on screen per part, shown as pictures. Time passing shown on a phone or wall clock.
