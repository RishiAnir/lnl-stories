# Lightning at Pine Ridge: story bible

## Premise
A dry lightning storm traps a first-season fire lookout in a mountain tower with a thru-hiker and the radio operator. The previous lookout, fired two years ago, has been secretly living in the tower's basement cistern room. He knows the mountain's hidden paths and the real reason the phone line is dead: a poaching crew is using the storm as cover to steal timber, and they've cut the fire road to keep witnesses away. Mystery first, then a wildfire escape. Second person: the viewer is "you," the new lookout. Three parts over one night.

## Characters
- **You**: early 20s, first season lookout. Khaki shirt with agency patch, binoculars on a chest harness, Osborne firefinder, radio headset. Observant, learning the job, notices details the veterans miss. Never named.
- **Hank**: 60s, radio operator / dispatcher. Voice on the radio for Part 1, climbs the emergency ladder in Part 2. Suspenders over a wool shirt, headset cord taped at the shoulder, thermos always in hand. Gruff, knows every peak by name, does the math on fire behavior out loud.
- **Jenna**: 30s, thru-hiker caught above treeline by the storm. Bright orange pack, wet Gore-Tex shell, trekking poles strapped to the catwalk rail. Practical, reads weather better than the forecasts, not afraid to question authority.
- **Elias**: 50s, former lookout fired for a "false alarm" that burned his credibility. Lives in the basement cistern room behind the water tank, coming up at night. Knows the tower's blind spots, the old telephone line route, the hidden cache of MREs and batteries. Cut the backup phone line himself when he saw the poachers' truck on the fire road. Not a villain: guilty, protective, and the only one who knows the way down. Seen only as a shape in Part 1; caught in Part 2.
- **The poacher crew** (Part 2+): two men in camo jackets, ATVs winched up the fire road, chainsaws, satellite phone. Never seen clearly: engine noise through the storm, headlights sweeping the trees, voices on the stolen agency frequency.

## Outline
1. **First Shift** (Day 1, 4 PM to 9 PM): you arrive for your first solo shift. Hank on radio walks you through the Osborne finder. Storm builds fast — dry lightning, no rain. Jenna climbs the tower stairs seeking shelter. The hardline phone goes dead. You check the basement for the junction box and find fresh cigarette butts beside the cistern. Someone's been down there. Cliffhanger: the Osborne finder's needle trembles on a bearing with no smoke — something metal flashing on the fire road below.
2. **The Basement** (9 PM to 2 AM): you, Jenna, and Hank (who climbed the emergency ladder) search the basement. Find Elias's nest: cot, practice maps with YOUR shift dates circled, a logbook of every lightning strike. Radio picks up voices on the agency frequency — not firefighters. Poachers. Elias steps from the shadows: "They cut the hardline. I cut the backup." He knows them. The beacon light fails. Total darkness. A chainsaw starts up below — they're cutting the fire road switchback, trapping you on the mountain. Cliffhanger: lightning illuminates smoke on the ridge. The storm just started the fire.
3. **Descent** (2 AM to dawn): fire crowns on the ridge, driven by storm winds. Poachers are burning a diversion to cover their escape with the timber. Elias leads you down the "telephone line trail" — a maintenance path only lookouts know, marked by porcelain insulators on trees. Hank coordinates from the cab, Jenna spots the poachers' ATVs stuck on the cut road. You reach the helipad as the fire crosses the saddle. Final twist: Elias stays at the radio to guide the helicopter in, redeeming the false alarm that got him fired. Rotor wash parts the smoke. Final image: the Osborne finder in the empty cab, needle steady on the smoke column.

## Story rules
- Open every part on its hook: the strangest or most dangerous fact, in the first sentence. Part 2 opens on the basement nest with your shift dates circled.
- Something happens every few seconds: actions, dialogue, radio static, reveals. No supply lists, no weather recaps, no backstory paragraphs.
- The characters talk. Hank gives orders and does fire math out loud in quotes, Jenna challenges and observes, Elias is evasive and guilty. Let them argue.
- A twist midway through each part, and a twist plus a question at the end.
- Survival detail only inside an event (winching the radio battery off the charger, not explaining how a repeater works).
- At most two numbers on screen per part, shown as pictures: time on the radio log, bearing on the Osborne dial, battery percentage.
- The tower is a character: guy wires singing in wind, beacon sweeping, stairs vibrating with thunder.
- No monsters. The fire is weather. The poachers are men. Elias is the ghost who knows the building.
