## Summary
The man in red reads rule four and walks into the last car despite June's pleas. The lights die; you keep your eyes shut and hear a ticket punch click in the dark. When the lights return, his seat is empty and his phone rings. You notice every newspaper is dated nineteen eighty seven. Then your own phone rings with Mom calling, and a voice whispers your name. Rule five tells you it isn't her. The train slows as the voice waits on the line.
