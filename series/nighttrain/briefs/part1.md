# Don't Miss Your Stop, Part 1: Wake Up
_Night 1, 2:14 AM_

Animator: follow `STYLE_LOCK.md` and reuse `assets/`. Build this part in `partN/` the same way as `part1/` (lines.json with chunks of 3 to 4 lines, film.js, mix.py, build.sh). Add any new assets below to the shared assets files and to STYLE_LOCK.md.

## Summary
You doze off on the train home and wake somewhere wrong: a silent car of passengers hidden behind newspapers, a phone stuck at 2:14, black windows, stops you've never heard of. An old woman is horrified to see you; she doesn't know where you are either. She introduces herself as June, talks with you, notices your stop isn't on the map, and gives you nine handwritten rules. The conductor punches tickets; a man in a red tracksuit nearly talks back and June saves him. June's own ticket is punched like lace. Then the man in red mocks the passengers, they copy him, he won't freeze, the lights go out, and he's gone. On the front page of a newspaper where he stood is a man in a red tracksuit.
