## Summary
You wake on a train where every passenger stares at you. Your phone is stuck at 2:14 AM with no signal. June gives you a handwritten list of rules. The conductor appears, punches your ticket, and moves on. June reveals her own ticket, punched so many times it is almost lace, warning you that each hole marks a missed stop. The lights flicker and the train shudders toward the next unknown station.
