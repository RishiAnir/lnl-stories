# Don't Miss Your Stop, Part 1: Wake Up
_Night 1, 2:14 AM_

## Summary
You doze off on the train home after a long day and wake somewhere wrong: a silent car of motionless passengers, black windows, stops you've never heard of, and a phone stuck at 2:14. An old woman across the aisle is horrified to see you, introduces herself as June and gives you a handwritten list of nine rules. The passengers copy your movements until you freeze; a man in a red tracksuit laughs it off; the conductor punches your ticket. June's own ticket has been punched hundreds of times.
